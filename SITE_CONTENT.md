# MP Doors & More - Site Content & Structure

## Project Overview
A professional home improvement supplier website for MP Doors & More in Sherman, TX. The site showcases doors, windows, flooring, siding, and shingles with a focus on quality materials at competitive prices.

## Design Philosophy
- **Color Scheme**: Deep navy/dark blue backgrounds with red accents (#C41E3A or similar), white text
- **Typography**: Bold, professional headings with clean body text
- **Layout**: Modern, asymmetric with hero imagery, product cards with hover effects
- **Key Visual**: Entryway/home improvement imagery, green dashed accent borders

## Design Elements

### Colors
- Primary Dark: #1a2332 or similar (navy)
- Accent Red: #C41E3A or similar
- Accent Green: #22c55e or similar (for borders/highlights)
- Text: White (#ffffff)
- Secondary Text: Light gray

### Typography
- Display Font: Bold, professional (e.g., Playfair Display, Montserrat Bold)
- Body Font: Clean, readable (e.g., Inter, Open Sans)

### Special Elements
- Green dashed borders on product cards and sections
- Hover effects on product cards (scale, shadow)
- Red buttons with white text
- Professional spacing and padding
- Responsive design for mobile/tablet/desktop
